      Historyflow

      Historyflow is a way of seeing how a document came to be: who wrote each part of it, when, and what became of it. It was invented by Fernanda Viégas and Martin Wattenberg at IBM Research in 2003, and described by them and Kushal Dave in Studying Cooperation and Conflict between Authors with history flow Visualizations (CHI 2004).

      This program is a new implementation of their idea, written by Jonathan Feinberg.
